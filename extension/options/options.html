● Draft Assistant

Your rankings drive everything. The panel inside ESPN's draft room always suggests the highest player on your list who's still available. It never reorders your list.

Your rankings

Import or replace your list

Paste from Excel/Google Sheets (copy the name column, plus the DR column if you use it), a CSV, or plain text. Name* = injury prone, DR = don't reach, Tier 3 lines start a tier. CSV columns: player, rank, tier, team, pos, flags, note (only player is required).

Scoring (for FP/G)

When ESPN sync works and your league is a points league, the panel uses ESPN's own scoring settings for your league automatically. These weights are the fallback.

Draft defaults

Used when ESPN can't be read (and on the manual board). ESPN sync overrides these with your league's real settings.

About ESPN's pre-draft rankings (autopick)

This extension only reads from ESPN. It doesn't edit ESPN's pre-draft list. ESPN has no published way to write that list, and silently changing your league data isn't something it should do. If you want autopick to follow your board too, open your league → Draft → Pre-Draft Rankings (Edit), and drag players into the order shown by Copy as text above. Usually only the first 3–4 rounds matter, since you'll be picking live.

Data: your list is stored in this browser only (chrome.storage). Live picks, ADP, injuries and projections come from ESPN while you're in the draft room and are never sent anywhere else.
